\subsection{BNPL Access, Peer Influence and Default}
\label{sec:results-rq2}

\label{sec:no-cascade}
The model has no feedback from household default to other households' income or to lenders' capacity to lend (Table~\ref{tab:design-concepts}).
It therefore cannot test contagion through those channels.
Peer influence can still produce abrupt adoption, and default may respond nonlinearly to the resulting obligations.
Whether that happens is an empirical question for the access and peer-influence sweeps, including the threshold variant.
