Table~\ref{tab:submodels} gives the rule and source for each submodel. This appendix holds the
complete numerical register: every parameter, its baseline value, its sweep range where it is
swept, and its provenance.

Three entries require flagging wherever they appear, and are marked in the register itself rather
than only in accompanying text.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Submodel~4, borrowing amount.} An assumption with no literature anchor. Carries
    mandatory sensitivity analysis over three specifications.
    \item \textbf{$q_{\text{base}}$ and $\beta$, Submodel~11.} Neither can be calibrated from any
    South African source. The $\beta = 0$ control arm is reported in every result.
    \item \textbf{Income-shock probability, Submodel~1.} Fitted to validation pattern~2, which is
    what makes the baseline calibrated rather than validated
    (Section~\ref{sec:lim-calibration}).
\end{itemize}
